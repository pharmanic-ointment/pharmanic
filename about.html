About us

Simple, dependable care in every jar.

Pharmanic is a brand of ointments for wound, skin and foot care, built around a small, purposeful range.

Who we are

A focused brand with a clear idea

We believe good care starts with clarity: knowing what a product is for and trusting it to do that job.

That is why Pharmanic keeps its range small. Each ointment addresses one specific concern, from open wounds and erysipelas to fungal infections, ingrown nails and hemorrhoids, and each is explained in simple, honest language.

The same attention goes into how the products look: a clean white jar, a matching box and an emblem that is instantly recognisable.

Mission & philosophy

What guides us

Our mission

To offer simple, dependable ointments that bring comfort and support to people dealing with wounds, skin problems and foot concerns.

Our philosophy

One ointment, one clear purpose. We would rather do a few things properly than many things vaguely.

Our values

The principles behind the range

01

Care

Our products are made for people at times when comfort matters most.

02

Clarity

We describe what each ointment is for in plain language, with no exaggeration.

03

From the contents of the jar to the box it comes in, details matter.

Our emblem

A symbol of continuous care

The Pharmanic emblem brings together three ideas that run through everything we make.

The interlaced knot

Unbroken, interwoven lines: care that is continuous and dependable.

Three swirls

White, red and golden ointment, a nod to the different kinds of care in the range.

Fresh leaves

Botanical touches that keep the identity fresh, calm and natural.
